#Monthly Business Health Reporter

Overview

The Monthly Business Health Reporter is a decision-support system that converts raw business data into clear, actionable insights for business owners.

This is not just a report generator. It is a business health monitoring engine designed to answer one core question:

> “Is my business actually healthy, and where do I need to act right now?”



The system analyzes monthly financial and operational data and produces a concise set of insights focused on risk, profitability, efficiency, and decision clarity.


---

What Problem This Solves

Most business owners:

Look at sales but miss profit leaks

Have data but no interpretation

React too late to expense growth

Don’t know which products or suppliers actually help profit


This engine fixes that by translating numbers into business meaning.


---

Core Outputs (Current Version)

The system generates 5 high-impact outputs that are intentionally limited to avoid information overload:

1. Business Health Status

Clear label such as Healthy, Stable, or At Risk

Based on revenue growth vs expense growth



2. Profit Impact Estimation

Shows potential downside or upside if trends continue

Helps owners understand future risk, not just past data



3. Top Performing Products

Identifies products driving the most profit

Helps owners focus on what actually works



4. Supplier Performance Insight

Highlights best and worst suppliers by margin impact

Supports negotiation and sourcing decisions



5. Executive Summary (Owner-Friendly)

Human-readable explanation of what the numbers mean

Focused on actions, not technical metrics




> These outputs are designed to be unavoidable and decision-oriented, not decorative analytics.
